Dog Sitting FAQs

Everything you might want to know before booking us to look after your dog.

Can you look after more than one dog?

Usually, yes! Tell us how many dogs you have and what they're like, so we can find the right sitter for your household.

What happens if something goes wrong?

We'll always contact the owner first if there's an issue. In an emergency, we'll follow the instructions you provide us and contact emergency services when necessary.

What information do you need from owners?

We'll ask for emergency contacts, allergies, medical needs, house rules, feeding instructions, and anything else we need to know to keep your dog safe and happy.
