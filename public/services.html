Services

Whatever your family needs — a little extra help with the children or some company for the dog — we're happy to lend a hand.

Child Sitting

We provide fun, creative and active time for all our young customers. Before any activities begin, we make sure every child feels safe and comfortable — and most importantly, that they have fun.

Our age range is from 1 to 12, and there are more details available on our app.

Pet Sitting

We can supply your dog with friendly visits, company, extra playtime and walks whenever they need a little extra attention while their family is out.

We also offer standalone dog walks at a lower price, without compromising on care.
