FAQs

General questions about who we are and how we work. Looking for something more specific? Check our Babysitting FAQs or Dog Sitting FAQs.

What ages do you babysit?

We're happy to babysit children from ages 1 to 12. Just let us know your children's ages so we can match you with the right sitter.

Do all three of you come to every booking?

Not always — while all three of us are happy to help, we don't all attend every single booking. We'll let you know in advance exactly who will be looking after your children or pets, so you always know who to expect.

Where do you provide your services?

We currently offer services in Molesey, Esher, Thames Ditton, Long Ditton and Walton. Please get in touch to check whether we cover your location.
