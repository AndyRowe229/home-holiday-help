Three friends. One big idea.

Home Holiday Help was founded by three teenage girls who wanted to turn their love of helping people, caring for children and spending time with animals into a business. We believe good help should feel friendly and dependable — and even the smallest acts of care can make a family's day much easier. We're proud to offer exceptional care at prices that are lower than most other holiday care providers.
