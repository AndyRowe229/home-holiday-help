Babysitting FAQs

Everything you might want to know before booking us to look after your children.

What do you do during babysitting sessions?

We can play games, help with homework, read stories, or do crafts — whatever keeps your children happy, safe and entertained.

Can you babysit in the evenings or on weekends?

Yes! Evening and weekend bookings are welcome, depending on availability. Take a look at our availability or get in touch to check a specific date.

Can you put my child to bed?

Absolutely — just give us their normal bedtime routine and any important information beforehand, and we'll follow it closely.

Can you prepare simple meals or snacks?

Yes, we're happy to prepare simple meals and snacks. Please let us know of any allergies or dietary requirements in advance so we can plan accordingly.

Can you accommodate allergies or dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Just give us the details before the booking and we'll make sure everything served is safe and suitable.
