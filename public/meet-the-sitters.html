Meet the Sitters

Home Holiday Help was founded by three friends who wanted to turn their love of helping people, caring for children and spending time with animals into a business. All three of us are happy to help with both dog sitting and child sitting.

Sienna

Sienna does both babysitting and dog sitting with a great passion for it. She has experience with long hours of babysitting and loves connecting with children, and has also done lots of dog walking.

Sophie

Sophie does both babysitting and dog sitting. She has plenty of experience and is always happy to lend a hand at home — she's very excited to help care for your dogs and children.

Lyla

Lyla does both dog and child sitting, with plenty of experience in babysitting and a genuine love for it. She also enjoys dog walking and dog sitting, and takes real pride in being reliable and attentive with every family she helps. She's always excited to lend a caring hand to local families, and loves getting to know the children and pets she looks after.
