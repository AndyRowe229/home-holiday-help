Meet the Sitters

Home Holiday Help was founded by three friends who wanted to turn their love of helping people, caring for children and spending time with animals into a business. All three of us are happy to help with both dog sitting and child sitting.

Sienna

Sienna does both dog and child sitting, with hands-on experience in dog walking, dog sitting and babysitting. She loves what she does and can't wait to help your family.

Sophie

Sophie does both dog and child sitting. She mostly focuses on dog sitting, but also enjoys dog walking and babysitting, and loves helping others.

Lyla

Lyla does both dog and child sitting, with plenty of experience in babysitting and a genuine love for it. She's always excited to lend a caring hand to local families.
