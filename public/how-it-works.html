How It Works

Booking Home Holiday Help is simple. Here's what to expect from your first message to the end of your booking.

  1. Get in touch

    Reach out through our app, or the contact page, and let us know what kind of help you're looking for — child sitting, dog sitting, or dog walking.

  2. Tell us about your family

    Share a few details about your children or dog, including ages, routines, and anything else we should know, so we can find the right match.

  3. We confirm your booking

    We'll check our availability and let you know exactly who will be looking after your children or pets before the booking begins.

  4. Sitting day

    We arrive on time and provide friendly, safe and responsible care, whether that's games, walks, homework help or bedtime routines.

  5. After the booking

    Once you've booked with us, we'll share our personal contact numbers for the services we complete, so you can always reach us directly.
